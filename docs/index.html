GOOGLE DISCOVER ARCHITECTURE

Why 90% of Web Stories Fail Google Audits

Google requires Web Stories at a 9:16 vertical ratio (1080×1920px). However, standard WordPress page builders ignore mobile screen chrome. Critical headlines get buried beneath system bars, and swipe-up CTAs get blocked by gesture navigation. Here is how our architecture guarantees full compliance.

Strict Safe Zone Optical Bounds (220px Top & 250px Bottom)

Google Web Stories injects native system UI, including the progress bar, story title, publisher logo, and audio mute controls, into the top 220px. At the bottom, Google overlays swipe-up link attachments and native Android/iOS home indicators across 250px. Our templates strictly confine headings, readable text, and tap targets to the central safe viewport, preventing Google Discover penalties and UI clipping.
