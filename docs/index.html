1-Click Blog-to-Story Distiller

Converts any published WordPress article into a punchy 4-slide AMP Web Story with key takeaways and swipe-up attribution outlink.

🎨

Unified Typography & Brand Harmony

Access 60+ Google Font families, custom font URLs, fluid headline sliders, and 1-click color palette synchronization across every slide.

🎵

Ambient Background Audio

Built-in royalty-free ambient audio loops (Lo-Fi Chill, Cinematic Pulse, Electronic Anthem) and custom MP3 soundtrack support.

Standalone & Official Plugin Sync

Works 100% standalone with native AMP HTML runtime, or integrates directly with Google's official Web Stories React editor.

Embed Shortcode Player

Social Story Slices Exporter

Render each slide to offscreen 1080×1920 vertical canvas and download high-res PNG images for Instagram Stories and TikTok.
